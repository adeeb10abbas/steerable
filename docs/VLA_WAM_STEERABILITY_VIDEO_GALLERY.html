VLA/WAM steerability study · two-arena direct-command pilots

Does changing one word redirect the robot?

One VLA and three world-action models. LEFT and RIGHT prompts are paired inside the same reset within each arena. These are complete success/failure rollouts—not a highlight reel.

What is held fixed? Within each pair: arena, native anchor task, environment seed, objects, initial poses, checkpoint, policy sampler, and action interface. Only the direction word and requested-relation checker change. There is no oracle, subtask coach, or prompt switching. DROID and RoboTwin scores are never pooled.
