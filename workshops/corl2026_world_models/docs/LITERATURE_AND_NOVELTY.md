# Literature positioning and novelty audit

Primary sources checked 12 September 2026. This is a bounded positioning review, not an exhaustive novelty certification. All recommendations below are interpretation. Existing historical results were supplied by the study team; this document does not independently revalidate those artifacts.

## Recommendation

Frame the research question as **whether the future jointly generated by a WAM is a trustworthy semantic witness of what its own executed action chunk will do under a specified goal**. The useful contribution is an auditable, horizon-aligned and predicate-aligned measurement protocol plus evidence that changes what a practitioner would infer. Do not present “good-looking video is not enough” as a discovery: several closely related papers explicitly establish that premise.

At present, the 80-episode/752-chunk Cosmos historical slice supports a measurement case study. It does not yet establish a model-level prediction failure rate. Its future scorer aggregates at least 75% of reliable frames, whereas execution uses one endpoint and a different vertical predicate. The eight off-diagonal cases mix model disagreement with scorer/horizon disagreement. They must remain historical *label mismatches*, pending aligned rescoring and blinded human adjudication.

The strongest paper would turn that limitation into a falsifiable result: quantify how apparent future/action agreement changes when temporal support, predicates, class prevalence, and visibility coverage are controlled, then show whether calibrated semantic evidence helps a held-out decision. Do not promise an award; current evidence alone is unlikely to distinguish the work from strong existing evaluation papers.

## What the historical numbers can and cannot say

The supplied labels contain 421 jointly classifiable chunks: 22 both positive, 5 future-only, 3 execution-only, and 391 both negative; 331 abstain. Arithmetic agreement is 413/421 = 98.1%, at 421/752 = 56.0% coverage. Positive evidence is sparse: only 30 classifiable chunks are positive on either side. An always-negative prediction of the execution label would score 396/421 = 94.1% under these same historical labels. Therefore high agreement is not strong evidence of useful positive prediction.

These are *descriptions of the old labels*, not newly validated future/action accuracy. Missing futures and unreliable perception are not negative outcomes. “Both negative” need not mean the same failure, the same attained state, or absence of task progress. Chunks are nested within episodes; 752 chunks must not be analyzed as 752 independent trials. Directional calibration and uncertainty must be estimated without using the held-out evaluation set to select thresholds.

## Minimum scientifically meaningful extension

1. **Make the pair real.** Audit chunk identifiers, output timestamps, action transforms, predicted-frame timing and actually executed prefix. If only half a predicted chunk executes before replanning, compare only corresponding future frames. Record censoring explicitly.
2. **Use the same semantic event.** Fix goal direction, object identity, containment/release criteria, camera geometry, temporal endpoint or interval, and visibility treatment before labels. If video cannot identify simulator-only facts, score a shared observable predicate and separately report privileged-state task success.
3. **Validate the judge.** Blind human annotators to model and old label; use an independently fixed rubric and two raters for a stratified sample spanning all outcome cells and abstentions. Include all eight mismatches for diagnostic review, but retain a random prevalence sample or sampling weights for population error estimates. Separate judge uncertainty from WAM uncertainty.
4. **Report informative denominators.** Provide the full confusion matrix, coverage, positive precision/recall, class-conditional errors and an episode-cluster bootstrap. Compare with always-negative, base-rate and current-state/persistence baselines. A future should add information beyond what the current scene already reveals.
5. **Replicate the finding.** Apply the frozen corrected protocol to an independent WAM or independent held-out cohort with decodable futures. Keep arenas/checkpoints separate. Neither missing interfaces nor infrastructure failure is zero performance.

## Extension most likely to elevate research value

Add a **held-out decision experiment** after measurement repair: does a semantic consistency signal choose between already available candidate WAM action chunks, or abstain from unsafe/incorrect chunks, better than a goal-success/value signal and simple baselines at matched action count and inference budget? Use an exact reset for paired alternatives where valid; disclose any proposal distribution or action intervention. Report achieved task outcomes against selection coverage and latency. Freeze the decision rule and thresholds before evaluating held-out episodes.

This converts an audit into evidence of practical utility. Do not infer causality from co-generation alone: correlation between video and action labels can arise because both reflect the prompt or initial scene. Comparing goal changes is a controlled language intervention, but it does not by itself test action-causal world modeling. An explicit action perturbation/replay experiment would test a different, stronger claim and needs an interface that actually supports it.

For a four-page research submission, prioritize one crisp result, one aligned protocol figure, one full denominator/confusion presentation, and one held-out extension. A broad literature survey or an eleven-model catalog would consume space without repairing the core inference.
