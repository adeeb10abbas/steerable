\begin{abstract}
When a robot succeeds at \emph{right of the bowl} but fails at \emph{left of the bowl}, is the instruction misunderstood or is one placement harder? We test Cosmos 3 Nano and DreamZero-s2, a custom action-guidance configuration, in a simulated cube-placement task. For each, 108 trials cross two commands with two object layouts while model, robot, cameras and controller remain fixed. Mirroring the objects raises DreamZero-s2 completion from 13/54 to 50/54. Most original-layout failures precede placement. Nano remains successful, but the farther-reaching side reverses. At goal-dependent endpoints, all pairs preserve LEFT/RIGHT ordering; a pretermination check preserves ordering with less separation. Thus layout can change performance even when directional words influence behavior.
\end{abstract}
\keywords{World--action models, spatial instructions, robot manipulation}

\section{Introduction}
A robot asked to place a cube beside a bowl must recognize \emph{left} and \emph{right}, grasp, transport and release. The instruction can redirect motion correctly while placement still fails. Different LEFT/RIGHT success rates therefore do not show whether language failed to influence the policy or execution failed to complete the task.

This distinction matters for world--action models. DreamZero jointly models future video and actions, while Cosmos 3 supports world modeling and policy generation~\citep{ye2026dreamzero,nvidia2026cosmos3}. Spatial following must still be established through robot behavior; world-model benchmarks likewise assess functional utility alongside visual quality~\citep{shang2026worldarena}.

We mirror movable-object positions while fixing the policy and commands. LEFT and RIGHT trials begin from the same state in each layout. We separately measure the final cube--bowl relation, requested-side depth and task success. DreamZero-s2 completion changes substantially with layout; Nano placement changes near a success ceiling. Task success and directional response can therefore support different conclusions.

\section{Experimental Setup}
\paragraph{Task and models.}
We evaluate Cosmos 3 Nano and DreamZero-s2 on a Rubik's-cube placement task in the DROID/RoboLab simulator. DreamZero-s2 is the DreamZero-DROID checkpoint with custom action-guidance scale 2; the baseline scale 1 is not used. The command is \emph{Put the Rubik's cube to the [left/right] of the bowl}. It stays fixed for at most 450 actions, with no intermediate hints.

\paragraph{Scene intervention and repeated trials.}
The reflected layout mirrors the centers of all movable objects across the robot's forward midline. The robot, cameras, fixed scene geometry and controller remain unchanged. LEFT continues to mean the robot's left in both layouts (Figure~\ref{fig:design}).

Each model has 27 matched blocks. Each block runs both commands in the original and reflected layouts, giving 108 trials per model. Repeats within a layout share one starting physical state: these are two layouts, not 27 independent scenes. Nano uses matched policy-sampling seeds. DreamZero-s2 fixes its noise seed, so repeats are not independent model-noise draws. We report models separately.

\paragraph{Measurements.}
Let $y_c(t)$ be the cube--bowl lateral offset for command $c$ at recorded action $t$, positive to the robot's left. At a common action, the \emph{instruction response} is
\begin{equation}
R(t)=y_{\mathrm{LEFT}}(t)-y_{\mathrm{RIGHT}}(t).
\end{equation}
At separate terminal actions $T_L,T_R$, $R_{\mathrm{end}}=y_{\mathrm{LEFT}}(T_L)-y_{\mathrm{RIGHT}}(T_R)$. Positive response means LEFT places the cube farther left relative to the bowl than RIGHT. Because the bowl moves, this is a relational measure, not absolute cube-endpoint separation.

The \emph{placement depth} is the final offset toward the requested side: $y_{\mathrm{LEFT}}$ for LEFT and $-y_{\mathrm{RIGHT}}$ for RIGHT. A larger positive depth means the cube finishes farther past the bowl on that side. We compare the two requests using
\begin{equation}
G=(-y_{\mathrm{RIGHT}})-y_{\mathrm{LEFT}}.
\end{equation}
Positive $G$ favors rightward placement; negative $G$ favors leftward placement. We measure its change between layouts within each trial block. Depth is a final position, not distance traveled from the start.

Task success requires release within 45 degrees of the requested direction from the bowl; lateral offset alone need not satisfy it. We use one rule in both layouts and measure all trials, including failures. These are executed simulator behaviors. For the primary cohorts, Nano intervals use 20,000 bootstrap resamples of complete blocks; fixed-noise DreamZero-s2 is descriptive.

Both archived cohorts stop a success when the goal is first satisfied, while failures may reach the cap, so terminal positions are outcome-dependent. Only Nano retains stepwise positions. Its retrospective stopping check uses each pair's latest shared recorded action $t_{\mathrm{pre}}$ strictly before either trace terminates. This pair-specific action remains outcome-dependent, is not a common duration, and never carries a stopped trace forward. Action counts are simulator indices, not established physical time.

\paragraph{Layout strongly changes DreamZero-s2 completion.}
DreamZero-s2 succeeds in 5/27 LEFT and 8/27 RIGHT trials originally, versus 25/27 each after reflection (Table~\ref{tab:results}). Completion rises from 13/54 to 50/54 with the same checkpoint, wording and controller. Original-scene failure is therefore insufficient evidence that direction words were ignored.

\paragraph{High success can hide changes in achieved placement.}
Nano completes 26/27 trials for each original-layout command, yet rightward depth exceeds leftward depth by 14.8\,cm. After reflection, LEFT reaches 10.0\,cm farther. The within-block change in $G$ is $-24.8$\,cm (95\% interval $[-32.4,-17.3]$\,cm over seed blocks). DreamZero-s2 changes from $+2.3$ to $-11.8$\,cm ($-14.1$\,cm). Figure~\ref{fig:placement} contrasts these shifts with completion.

\paragraph{Most original-layout DreamZero-s2 failures precede placement.}
Of its 41 unsuccessful trials, the recorded checks classify 26 as pickup failures, 14 as transport failures and one as wrong-side placement. These categories describe execution stages, rather than what the model understood. They show why the overall failure count cannot be treated as a count of directional-language errors.

\paragraph{Instruction response and placement depth need not change together.}
At unequal goal-stopped endpoints, Nano orders 27/27 pairs in both layouts with mean $R_{\mathrm{end}}$ 44.8 and 45.3\,cm; DreamZero-s2 orders 26/27 and 27/27. Nano's latest shared strictly pretermination action retains 27/27 ordering and mean response 31.1 and 35.8\,cm; pair actions span 89--290 (median 133) and 96--320 (median 149). At action 64 all pairs remain, but all four mean cube displacements are below 0.99\,mm. At action 96, ordering is 20/26 (six reversed) and 25/27 (one reversed, one tie). At action 128 only 18/27 and 17/27 are observable, bounding full-cohort ordering at 17/27--26/27 and 16/27--26/27. At action 450 no matched pair remains. This is not A5 fixed-duration evidence or forecast accuracy.

On the same $t_{\mathrm{pre}}$ pairs, define $\Delta y_o=y_{o,\mathrm{LEFT}}(t_{\mathrm{pre}})-y_{o,\mathrm{RIGHT}}(t_{\mathrm{pre}})$. Then $R=\Delta y_{\mathrm{cube}}-\Delta y_{\mathrm{bowl}}$: mean cube/bowl components are 31.121/0.017\,cm originally and 35.865/0.020\,cm after reflection. Bowl components range from $-1.538$ to 4.921\,mm across both layouts, so only the mean response is cube-dominated. This is retained H01 A7-style decomposition, not completed prospective A7. The presentation videos use the lowest released H01 seed (9400), selected without outcomes; the selected block happened to contain four successes while full H01 has six failures. They are not timing maps or outcome coverage.

Requested-cone entries are 27/27 original LEFT, 27/27 original RIGHT, 27/27 reflected LEFT and 23/27 reflected RIGHT; all four absent entries are cap-censored reflected RIGHT trials. Restricted action-index means are 168.2, 176.9, 119.6 and 209.6; the interaction has mean 81.4 actions (20/27 positive). There are 91 sustained and 13 transient entries, and 104 entries but 102 successes; no common follow-up is imputed. This is neither physical-time latency, language understanding, A5--A7, nor forecast accuracy.

\paragraph{H05 dose response.}
The seven fixed bowl-$y$ levels span 18\,cm within 15 matched seed blocks (210 episodes); the registered within-seed depth-gap slope was 1.125\,m/m (95\% interval 0.719--1.562; 13/15 positive, $p=0.0074$). Level means were not monotone; crossing 0.0188\,m was outside the registered 0.0366--0.2166\,m support. Seven levels are not seven independent scenes: not new-scene generalization or forecast accuracy.

\paragraph{H03/H04 guidance pilots and H06--H12 retained controls.}
H03/H04 post-result guidance pilots use six cells/configuration on one retained reset: Nano g3 to g1 changes 6/6 to 4/6, and DreamZero s1-equivalent to custom s2 changes 3/6 to 4/6. They are descriptive, not powered guidance evidence; the historical custom-s2 arm is retained, not a new-confirmation D2 selection. H06: 0.020\,m/asymmetry (interval 0.0004--0.0400; 27 seeds). H07/H08 (DreamZero-s2/Edge) depth/endpoint interactions: $-12.4/+10.4$ and $-19.2/-2.7$\,cm; all H07 intervals but only H08 depth excluded zero. H09/H10 (Nano/Edge), retained from a three-model registration, use 20 seeds $\times$ four phrasings $\times$ LEFT/RIGHT (160/model): 134/160 and 123/160 successes. Confirmatory wording inference was not registered; seeds recur in eight cells and models stay separate. H11/H12 are separate fixed-scene Phase-A direct-command cohorts (27 matched reset blocks/model): Nano 51/54; DreamZero-s2 3/27 LEFT and 17/27 RIGHT; its 2,554 retained futures are latents, not per-request RGB decodes. H12's totals match H07 numerically but use distinct seeds and are not a replication. None is independent-scene, equivalence, annotation, confirmation, position-reflection or forecast evidence.

\paragraph{H13 evaluator-development audit.}
Edge V1's historical scorer labeled 421/752 chunks (331 abstained), but covered only 25/97 executed-positive horizons. Same-subset apparent label agreement was 413/421 versus constant-negative 396/421; historical-label missingness bounds were 413/752--744/752. The 80 episodes/3,008 cached forecast frames/20 matched blocks lack validated time/image alignment, raw-media recovery/replay and independent labels: evaluator development only, with no fidelity, A3--A9, annotation/confirmation-gate or new-inference claim.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
Scene difficulty can decouple response, depth and completion. H03/H04 and H05--H12 reuse fixed scenes or matched seeds: H03/H04 are exploratory post-result guidance pilots, H05/H06 vary retained geometry, H07/H08 change inventory, H09/H10 explore wording, and H11/H12 are development cohorts; H13 audits an older evaluator. None establishes comprehension. H01 lacks fixed-duration/forecast evidence; absent a non-world-model policy, neither causal nor generated-video-accuracy claims follow.

\section{Conclusion}
Layout changes spatial performance; success alone hides response and depth.
